## Product Overview
The BMG-LASER-1 laser marking machine uses high-energy laser beams to generate permanent markings on material surfaces through physical or chemical reactions. It is suitable for industrial coding and identification applications.

## Features
- Supports text, number, graphic, and texture marking
- High-speed laser marking operation
- Permanent and high-precision marking effect
- Suitable for food, packaging, pharmaceutical, and electronics industries
