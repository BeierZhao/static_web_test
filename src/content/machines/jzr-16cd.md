## Product Overview
The JZR-16CD valve bag vacuum shaping packing machine is designed for ultrafine powders and lightweight powder materials. It integrates vacuum filling, shaping, and sealing functions for high-efficiency dust-free packaging.

## Features
- Full vacuum packaging removes internal air effectively
- Sealed packaging process minimizes dust leakage
- Integrated automatic bag opening and vacuum system
- Suitable for lightweight fine powder materials
