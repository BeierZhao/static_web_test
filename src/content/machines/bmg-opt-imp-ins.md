## Product Overview
The BMG-VISION Optical Impurity Inspection Machine uses optical transmission, reflection, refraction, and scattering technology to automatically detect impurities and foreign objects in products.

## Features
- Automatic impurity and foreign object detection
- Detects particles, dust, metal fragments, hair, bubbles, and haze
- High-speed optical inspection process
- Suitable for food, pharmaceutical, semiconductor, and fine chemical industries
