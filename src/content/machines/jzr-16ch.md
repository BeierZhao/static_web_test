## Product Overview
The JZR-16CH auger valve bag packing machine is designed for powder and granular material packaging. It uses auger feeding technology for accurate and stable bag filling in industrial packaging applications.

## Features
- Variable frequency auger feeding system
- Stable and accurate powder filling
- Compatible with paper bags, woven bags, and composite bags
- Suitable for chemical, food, pharmaceutical, and mineral materials
