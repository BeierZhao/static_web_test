## Product Overview
The JZR-50A valve bag shaping machine is designed for reshaping filled bags into square forms after packaging. It improves bag appearance and stacking stability during transportation and storage.

## Features
- Automatic bag shaping and flattening
- Square bag forming for stable stacking
- Compatible with vacuum packaging systems
- Integrated pneumatic and variable frequency control system
