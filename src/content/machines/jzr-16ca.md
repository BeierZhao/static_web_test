## Product Overview
Features both pneumatic feeding and screw feeding modes, specially designed for powders with high air content and low bulk density such as PVC, ground calcium carbonate, and carbon black. The full screw version is suitable for viscous materials, while the semi-screw version is ideal for fine powders with good flowability ranging from 325 to 2500 mesh.

## Features
- Pneumatic sealing and ultrasonic sealing
- Automatic bag pushing and placing device
- Air return dust collection system for a clean workshop environment
